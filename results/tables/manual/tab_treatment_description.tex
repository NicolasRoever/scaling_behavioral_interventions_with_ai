\textbf{General interview style:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep]
\item MI-consistent behavior 
\item Redirects sustain talk towards change talk
\item No active attempt at inducing ambivalence about behavioral change
\item Reflections and summaries are biased towards participants’ change-oriented statements
\end{itemize}
\end{minipage}

\begin{minipage}[t]{\linewidth}\vspace{0pt}
\textbf{General interview style:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep]
\item MI-consistent behavior
\item No active attempt to mitigate sustain talk
\item Explores both pros and cons of behavioral change (decisional-balance)
\item Two-sided reflections and summaries that give equal weight to both positives and negatives
\end{itemize}
\end{minipage}
&
\vspace{-0.5em}
\begin{minipage}[t]{\linewidth}\vspace{0pt}\raggedright
\textbf{General interview style:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep]
\item Asks leading, closed-ended questions emphasizing need to change
\item Actively counter-argues
\item Actively pushes the participant to commit to reducing social media use
\item Reflections and summaries include comments and judgment from the interviewer
\end{itemize}
